How it works

Real people. Real proximity.
Real plans.

No feed to scroll, no algorithm picking for you. Just the people around you and what they're up to right now.

1

Set your status

Say what you're up to — "Coffee anyone?", "New in town", "Anyone up for a walk?" It's an open invitation.

2

See who's nearby

Browse the people around you, sorted by distance. See their status at a glance and just say hi.

3

Make it happen

Message someone directly or join an open invitation — then actually meet up. That's the whole point.

Features

Built for real-world plans

Everything you need to turn "someone's nearby" into "we're doing something" — nothing you don't.

Proximity-based

People sorted by real distance — the ones you can actually meet today, not a feed from across the world.

Open invitations & events

Post something you want to do and let people join, or hop into an event happening nearby. Plans, not just chat.

Group & 1-on-1 chat

Message anyone nearby, or talk in a group around an event. Conversations that turn into plans.

Private & safe

Location only while the app is open, no background tracking, no data selling — ever. Moderation and reporting keep it respectful. 18+.
